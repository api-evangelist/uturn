# UTURN

> UTURN Entertainment is a Saudi Arabian digital media and entertainment company founded in 2010, grown into the largest Arabic Multi-Channel Network (MCN) in the MENA region. It produces original digital content — sketches, gaming, lifestyle, and sports shows — across YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, and other social platforms, and offers content creation, sponsored content, branded content, and digital production services. UTURN publishes no public API or developer surface.
